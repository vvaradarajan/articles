# Event Driven Task  
## Look at the following picture:  
<img src="img/lessons/threeEvents.jpeg">  
You will observe three things pointed out:  1. sock, 2. a paper bag and 3. a glass cup with tea remnants.  
These are three event, and each of them should trigger a task:  
Task 1: put the sock near the shoe rack.  
Task 2: put the paper bag in the recyclable trash can.  
Task 3: put the cup in the sink

<b>Concept</b>: This is a house organizing venture. Gd around the house observing disorganized events. This part is difficult, and hopefully you know this by prior learning. Then complete the associated task. This algorithm is probably the most efficient and the least amount of steps (no list of things to be done, plan etc. - Just do it!)
