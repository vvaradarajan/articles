**Other conditions that disturb sleep that I noticed:**

1. All doors and windows closed - cannot sleep. (Supposed Reason: Bad air quality => too much cO2 which creates stress and wakefulness is so that you can breathe. Also the still air make make the cO2 you breathe out stay close to you.
2. Eating too close to bedtime. The start digestion is disturbing to sleep.
3. Cool body/skin helps: A shower before bed, cool sheets.
4. Dust can disturb sleep. (watch for ceiling fans that blow dust. Have a side fan or window fan is best)

**Equipment you need for reduced sleep Apnea:**

1. Window or side fan or better an air purifier to blow air across the bed. (about $40)
2. Air quality monitor (about $40)
3. Tongue sucker (about $8)
4. The spO2 meter with watch type and probe attached to the finger => wellvue or vitality. These are most accurate and accepted as clinically accurate. This records the spO2 overnight and without charging for several nights. (about $100)
5. Cool mattress topper, blankets (Bamboo topper is good: about $40)

**Actions you can take:**

1. Cool shower - short (or be cool and skin clean) sometime before bed.
2. Any food should be consumed minimum 1-2 hrs before bed.
3. Reduce any stress, turn off lights, TV.
4. Monitor your spO2 recordings each day and observe/relate to anything you can change/control.
